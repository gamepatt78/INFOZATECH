# Task 2 — Product Search App

Sharing my Product Search App, created as part of my frontend development internship with InfozaTech!

This responsive app makes it easy to explore a public product catalog: search by keyword, filter by category, sort results, and open a product for more details. The catalog excludes vehicles and motorcycles. I built the interface with React and Vite and connected it to public product data.

In my demo video, I’ll show the search, category filters, sorting options, and product details.
