# Task 4 — Drag-and-Drop Kanban Board

Here’s my Kanban Board project, built as part of my frontend development internship with InfozaTech!

The board helps organize work across To Do, In Progress, and Done. Users can add, edit, and delete cards, move them between columns with drag and drop or built-in controls, and keep their board after refreshing the page.

In my demo video, I’ll show how to manage a card, move it between columns, and see the board state persist.
