# Task 1 — Interactive Analytics Dashboard

Excited to share my Interactive Analytics Dashboard, built as part of my frontend development internship with InfozaTech!

Northstar Store Analytics brings key retail insights together in a responsive dashboard. It includes sales summaries, interactive charts, filters, product rankings, and a recent-orders view. I built it with React, Vite, Recharts, and Firebase Authentication.

The dashboard currently uses illustrative sample data, rather than live store or customer records. In my demo video, I’ll walk through the dashboard and show how its filters and visualizations work.
